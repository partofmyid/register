# Website & Usage Requirements

This document outlines the requirements for registering subdomains. Things like minimum website content, allowed subdomain uses, etc. Feel free to open an issue if you have any questions or concerns about these requirements.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 1. The part-of.my.id organization is based in Indonesia, including all of our available apex domains. Therefore, all registered subdomains must comply with Indonesian laws and regulations.
> 2. We (maintainers) reserve the right to reject registration PRs and take down existing subdomains if we find violations of the following requirements.
> 3. If you find any subdomain that violates these requirements, please open an issue to report it along with the violating subdomain(s).

## Eligibility

- Organizations are not allowed to register subdomains. Must be an individual.
- Provide truthful and accurate information during registration.

## Website Requirements

Your website **MUST** meet the following requirements:
- In production, live, and publicly viewable.
- Completed and not contain any placeholder content.
- All root/not nested subdomains must be a personal website/portfolio.

Your website **MUST NOT** contain the following:
- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination
- Promotion of violence and harm
- Hoax or misleading information
- Scams, phishing, or fraud activities
- Illegal content hosting
- Malware distribution
- Copyrighted material
- Commercial/for-profit purposes
- Cryptocurrency/blockchain
- DDOS/Abusive automation 
- Proxies or VPN services
- AI Chatbot websites
- Game cheats/hacks/exploits
- Violations of Indonesian laws and regulations
